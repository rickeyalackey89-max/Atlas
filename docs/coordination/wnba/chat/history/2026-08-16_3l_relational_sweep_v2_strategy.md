# 2026-08-16 — 3L Relational Sweep V2 Strategy Checkpoint

## What ran

Four predeclared current-stack relational architectures were evaluated across 30 applicable 3L dates and 1,609 candidates using target-date-excluded outer LODO.

The official action-level result for all four arms was 20-9-1.

## Why the official conclusion requires qualification

The V2 experiment combined a learner with a nested override gate.

The gate frequently chose `INF`, which means KEEP incumbent regardless of the held-out challenger's margin.

At least two control-loss dates demonstrate the distinction:

- 2026-07-06: V2-A/D chose the exact best-ranked winning challenger; gate kept the losing incumbent.
- 2026-07-31: V2-A/B/D chose the exact best-ranked winning challenger; gate kept the losing incumbent.

The official comparative logic also defined `RELATIONAL_SIGNAL_EXISTS_BUT_UNSTABLE` as requiring both a discrimination signal and at least one **acted** beneficial override. This made the signal classification dependent on gate behavior.

## Current interpretation

Do not promote a V2 method.

Do not call relational learning proven.

Also do not call relational signal absent.

The correct next scientific task is a cheap, no-refit gate-decomposition audit of the already-sealed OOS challenger outputs before spending additional hours on a new learner.

Potential result classes for that future audit should distinguish:

- learner did not identify winning challengers;
- learner identified winners but threshold transfer failed;
- learner identified winners only sporadically/randomly;
- a stable pre-gate challenger pattern exists and deserves a newly predeclared gate experiment.
