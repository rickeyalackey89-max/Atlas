# WNBA Protected Validation Doctrine

Status: **ACTIVE STRATEGIC DOCTRINE**

## Public/live outputs

Public or live slips are operational outputs only.

They are **never** validation, lockbox, promotion, or statistical authority and must never substitute for controlled protected evaluation.

## Builder sequencing

Current intended Builder sequence:

1. freeze 2L research method;
2. freeze 3L research/depletion method;
3. research and freeze 4L on the exact post-3L residual surface;
4. research and freeze FromDeep as its independent specialist family;
5. inventory protected unevaluated dates without opening outcomes;
6. evaluate the frozen methods on protected validation dates;
7. if and only if the methods remain unchanged after validation, open the final lockbox once.

## Validation and lockbox remain separate

Validation is the first protected evaluation gate. If validation causes any method, gate, threshold, depletion, ranking, family, or architecture change, that validation evidence is consumed for development and the lockbox remains sealed.

The lockbox is a later one-shot confirmation gate, not an extension of iterative tuning.

## New unevaluated dates

A newer date may be admitted to a future protected evaluation pool only when all of the following are proven without outcome peeking:

- the relevant pregame candidate surface/state existed and was sealed before outcome;
- the method state or inputs required for evaluation are reconstructible without outcome leakage;
- the date outcome has not been consumed in Builder/model development;
- the date is assigned to validation or lockbox before its outcome is opened for evaluation.

Do not infer protected eligibility merely because a date has not yet been formally scored.

## Evidence language

Historical-as-of development evidence remains development evidence even when causally clean. Protected validation and lockbox are separate authorities.

This doctrine does not itself authorize opening validation or lockbox.
